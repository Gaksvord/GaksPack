# Mob texture credits

The mob catalog includes adaptations of the following resource packs. Original
archives are not modified; the catalog contains per-mob adaptations.

- **Gray's Mob Overhaul** and **Updated Mobs** by CanineGray. Included with the
  author's permission; the original license is retained in the individual packs.
- **Mobs Refreshed** by lukidonu. Split into per-mob packs with one model variant,
  included with the author's permission. [Original project](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/mobs-refreshed).
- **ReCrafted Mobs** by GillsBills. Split into per-mob packs with one model variant.
  The original pack also credits Cave Fox, kekkman, PinkuRinku, RavenVLemon,
  Ewanhowell5195 and MickeyJoeAlpha. The adaptations are licensed under
  [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
  [Original project](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/recrafted-mobs).
